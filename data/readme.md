The Robusta experiment saves the following two result files directly in the `data/` directory:

- `satisfiable_predicates.txt`: Contains the satisfiable TLP variants.
- `experiment_summary.txt`: Contains the final RL graph node/edge counts, executability and satisfiability rates, mean similarity, population standard deviation, and predicate generation speeds for similarity thresholds of ≤40% and ≤80%.

The standalone LLM and L-error experiments save their results separately in `data/LLM_results/` and `data/L-error_results/<experiment>_results/`, respectively.
